The **Responsible and Safe AI Lab** is led by [Associate Professor Vinay Chakravarthi Gogineni](https://vinaychakravarthi.github.io/) at the Applied Artificial Intelligence and Data Science Unit, The Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Institute, University of Southern Denmark.

We build AI that is not only accurate, but also private, fair, transparent and dependable, and we study how AI can run efficiently on emerging quantum hardware. Our work rests on three core pillars, **foundational AI**, **responsible AI** and **quantum AI**, with applications in healthcare, the industrial internet of things, life sciences and optics.
