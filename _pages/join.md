We are open to collaborations with both academia and industry. If you are interested in partnering on research projects or innovative AI applications, please reach out to [Vinay Chakravarthi Gogineni](mailto:vigo@mmmi.sdu.dk).

**Postdocs.** For candidates with a background in AI or large-scale optimization, we encourage you to review our recent work. If it aligns with your interests and you wish to join the team, please send your CV. While fully funded positions may not always be available, we (our unit) will support your fellowship applications to Danish and European funding agencies.

**PhD students.** Interested candidates are welcome to enquire about applying for funding.

**Master and Bachelor students.** You are welcome to enquire about thesis and project opportunities.

**Visiting students.** We welcome and support international students visiting via the [DDSA Visiting Grant](https://ddsa.dk/visitgrant/).
